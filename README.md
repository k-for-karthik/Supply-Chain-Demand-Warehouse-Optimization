# 📦 Supply Chain Demand & Warehouse Optimization

[You can access the project files here](https://github.com/k-for-karthik/Supply-Chain-Demand-Warehouse-Optimization)<br>
### 🧠 Business Problem
The company struggled with stock imbalances across regions and warehouses. Frequent stockouts and inefficient restocking were increasing costs and customer dissatisfaction.

### 📊 Data & Tools Used
- **Data Size:** 50,000+ inventory and logistics records
- **Tools:** `Python`, `Excel`

### 🔍 Approach
1. Cleaned and prepared inventory, order, and warehouse data.
2. Analyzed regional demand distribution and stock shortage frequency.
3. Reviewed restocking intervals by warehouse.
4. Performed seasonal trend analysis to forecast demand peaks.

### 💡 Key Findings
- **Asia & North America** had the highest demand; **Africa** had the lowest.
- **WH05, WH06, WH11** showed the highest stock shortage risks.
- **June–July** were peak demand months.
- Warehouses like **WH11** required more frequent restocking (every 49 days).

### 📈 Impact & Outcome
- Reduced stockouts by **~20%** by adjusting inventory allocations.
- Lowered emergency replenishments by **35%** through optimized restocking schedules.
- Improved customer satisfaction with fewer delivery delays and backorders.
